# Evidence-led performance reviews and experiments

Editorial desk → Performance evidence now provides private descriptive diagnostics
from already retained observations. It does not query a provider, change existing
Studio automation, select a winning treatment, or execute paid/public actions.

## Comparability and missing data

Each content item is compared with **other** items from the same platform,
account, native format and publication-age window: 24h, 72h, 7d or 30d, within
±10%. An unspecified account remains a separate owner-import cohort. Each item
contributes its nearest-age observation; an exact-distance tie uses the later
reporting boundary, then its stable evidence key. Revised/superseded content
mapping semantics are shared with the audience adapter and cohort summary.

Peer counts are metric-specific. Click-through is computed only from available,
valid clicks and positive impressions on the same observation; no missing metric
becomes zero. Rewatch percentage can exceed100. Booleans/numeric strings cannot
be imported as floating-point measurements. Comparisons show values, peer counts,
medians and missing/small-sample/zero-baseline states.

Five other items and a subject/peer-median ratio below0.75 produce a **review
heuristic**, not a significance result or causal explanation. Lower impressions,
lower click-through and lower aggregate viewing are distinguished when their
respective measures exist. Suggestions propose a bounded creative experiment.
An aggregate average cannot identify an opening drop-off. Repeat-viewer behavior,
detailed retention, topic/length/packaging/timing confounders and reporting latency
remain explicit unknowns. Views alone cannot diagnose distribution or packaging.

## Cost context

A current exact content mapping may supply its retained variable-cost snapshot.
Only known cost bases, a nonnegative recorded amount, no unsettled request at that
snapshot and a positive view denominator yield a per1000 ratio. Engaged views are
preferred; ordinary views are distinctly labeled. Missing costs stay unavailable,
including unlinked/legacy work with no reliable attribution. Very small positive
amounts display as less than a threshold instead of rounding into an apparent
zero. These are mapping-time estimates/owner attestations, excluding fixed costs;
they are neither current invoices, incremental production prices, profit nor ROI.
Current billing/holds must be reconciled before a budget decision.

## Explicit decisions and experiments

A private plan/defer/reject decision requires substantive rationale and an
acknowledgment bound to its exact measures, peer evidence, source mapping, cost
snapshot and heuristic policy. Stale evidence returns409 while preserving owner
words. Decisions are append-only in SQLite/PostgreSQL; exact retries recover the
same receipt. History retains the reviewed diagnostic. A previous decision is
visibly marked when it no longer matches current evidence. No decision grants
spend, replies, publication or automation changes.

Only the latest current plan decision can prefill an editable hypothesis and success measure.
The owner supplies distinct control/variant IDs and confounders. Registration
retains the exact learning decision/version and requires its original platform,
account, format and age; changed evidence or a superseding decision requires a new review. Exact registration
retries recover the same experiment. Results identify selected observation keys,
keep per-group counts/medians and remain descriptive with no automatic winner.
Unknown publication timing is labeled unverified; an experiment is only labeled
prospective once all selected publication times establish that ordering.

The report and history are private/paginated. Expensive peer comparisons are
built only for the requested page or one reviewed item. Filtering by content ID
and explicit refresh aid larger histories. Decisions can be saved as browser
drafts; failed storage participates in the existing navigation protection.

## Verification limits

Tests use original synthetic performance observations and actual local HTTP/SQL/UI.
Real private provider access, attribution completeness, invoice reconciliation,
causal experimentation and beneficial audience outcomes remain separate proof.
The advisory policy is deliberately non-executing. It does not add another live
scheduler, enable unattended publishing or claim that growth has been achieved.
