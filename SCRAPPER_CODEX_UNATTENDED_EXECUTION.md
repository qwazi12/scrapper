2. Overall product outcome: Screen Central OS
The goal is an end-to-end mostly autonomous, editorially controlled media growth system, not simply a video downloader, prompt wrapper, or posting scheduler:
- Audience intelligence: own-channel performance and history, vidIQ signals, competitor outliers, searches, viewer questions, topic timing and confidence-aware opportunities. Never equate AI guess scores with measured demand.
- Editorial discovery: choose a distinct audience, promise, format, original angle, evidence plan and reason to produce this idea instead of blindly accepting a TMDB trending title.
- Factual research: independent source verification, structured claim provenance, title/year matching, factual contradictions, freshness, uncertainty and escalation. No self-referential LLM fact checking.
- High-quality creative production: strong narrative and retention, narration and audio quality, visually appropriate footage, shot/caption synchronization, contrast/legibility, no misleading or repetitive visuals, distinctive packaging, verified thumbnails, real final-file inspection.
- Multiple native formats: long-form YouTube, original Shorts and Instagram/Facebook Reels, 9:16 recuts, carousels/image posts, community questions/polls and campaign-specific metadata. Reuse research, NOT automatic identical posts or low-effort copying.
- Safe scheduling and distribution: distinct provider-specific metadata and assets, idempotent independent platform attempts, reconciliation, unknown-state handling, human approval gates and reliable audit logs.
- Audience engagement: analyze comments/requests/corrections, propose thoughtful personalized replies, turn evidence into future content, keep human approval for public actions.
- Learning loop: tie each publish ID back to the content campaign, compare same-format and same-age results, distinguish low impressions / weak packaging / poor opening / retention / lack of repeat viewers, recommend the next experiments, never invent performance metrics.
- Rights-aware asset intelligence: reuse and index the owner-owned and appropriately licensed media library, organize metadata/captions/scene semantics/provenance, integrate approved/official sources through modular connectors. Learn architectural concepts from Stremio/Mangayomi/NewPipe, but do not blindly integrate pirated repositories, torrents, ripping or DRM bypass.
Existing code includes Python/FastAPI backend, Next.js frontend, Railway backend, Vercel frontend, SQLite volume, TMDB/IMDb/Gemini/vidIQ/TTS/yt-dlp/FFmpeg, LongForm Studio, Countdowns Studio, queue/SocialPilot, Google Drive and optional local worker. Maintain working capabilities, owner customizations and undo/recovery semantics. Avoid SaaS distractions.
